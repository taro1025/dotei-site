DOTEI

There is no road ahead of me.
Behind me, a road is made.

Kōtarō Takamura, “Dōtei (The Journey)”

DOTEI is an iPhone app that draws only the streets you have walked, in ink, on a blank map.

  • Import years of past walks from Google Maps Timeline, Health workouts and GPX files
  • Streets you walk again grow darker; streets you walk for the first time turn vermilion
  • Pick a year to see only the streets you walked that year
  • Your records stay on your iPhone. No account needed
  • Free for the first 7 days, then a one-time purchase (no subscription)
