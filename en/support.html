Importing from Google Maps

  1. Open Google Maps on your iPhone and tap your profile picture at the top right
  2. Open Settings > Personal content (or Location & privacy)
  3. Tap Export Timeline data and choose Save to Files
  4. In DOTEI, go to Walks > Import > Google Maps Timeline > Choose a file

Records.json and the monthly Semantic Location History files from Google Takeout work too. If you have a .zip, unzip it in the Files app first.

Samples to try: Timeline.json and walk.gpx (fictional walks)

If no Health workouts are found

Go to Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > DOTEI and turn on Workouts and Workout Routes. Routes are saved for outdoor walks, runs, hikes and rides.

Recording with the screen off

Keep location access at “While Using the App”, tap Start walking, then lock your phone. The location indicator shows while recording.

Restoring purchases

Settings > Restore Purchases.

Deleting records

Delete walks one by one from Walks, or all at once in Settings > Delete all records. Records exist only on this iPhone.
